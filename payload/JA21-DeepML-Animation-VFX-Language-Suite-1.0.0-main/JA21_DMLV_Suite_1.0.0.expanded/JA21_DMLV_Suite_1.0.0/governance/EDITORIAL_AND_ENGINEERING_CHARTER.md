# Editorial and Engineering Charter

## SOPHIA

Semantic integrity, mathematical claim boundaries, and terminology governance.

## CHARLOTTE

Information architecture, taxonomy, discoverability, and editorial consistency.

## LANDON

Compiler/runtime contracts, validation, replay, and deterministic packaging.

## Professor

Curriculum sequencing, exercises, assessment, and textbook pedagogy.

## Podium

Publication quality, release presentation, catalog design, and navigable delivery.

## Shared release rules

- Preserve `deepml.vfx` identity and source provenance.
- Maintain deterministic, no-network, stable-ID, and no-unknown-execution policies.
- Distinguish syntax, type, semantic, safety, runtime-validation, and certification outcomes.
- Do not collapse projected visual overlap into latent geometric or semantic coupling.
- Treat optimizations as evidence-bearing transformations.
- Keep paths short and Windows-safe.
